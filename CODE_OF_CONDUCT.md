# Code of Conduct

InkStack contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and safe.

- Be considerate and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome contributors regardless of experience or background.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose another person's private information.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about project scope, safety, and moderation.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
